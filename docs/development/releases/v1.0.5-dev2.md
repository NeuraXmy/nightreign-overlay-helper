## 中文

- 调整自动恢复失败提示：同一玩家在当前运行中只提示一次，并显示明确的恢复失败状态；重启后恢复正常待命，不再重复展示历史失败，记录仍保留。
- 问题反馈 ZIP 新增 P2PHelper 的恢复事务、停止记录、状态及请求结果，补充辅助程序运行日志，方便排查 Steam 游戏运行状态异常。

## English

- Show automatic recovery failure alerts once per player during the current session. Restarting returns the helper to normal standby without replaying historical failures; diagnostic records are retained.
- Include P2PHelper recovery transactions, stopped records, status, requests and results in feedback ZIPs, with additional helper logs to investigate unexpected Steam game activity.
