# Overview

Enable this page’s switch, **Co-op Status**, and the **Circle Timer** to show the commendation window automatically when teammates leave.

1. Teammates are recorded for the current run after DAY1 is detected.
2. Each teammate appears when they leave, including mid-game disconnections. Friends staying in the lobby do not affect prompts for other teammates.
3. Click a name to open its profile in Steam; click 👍 on the right to post the comment saved on this page.
4. The window is shown for 120 seconds by default, adjustable to 10–600 seconds. Click × to close it immediately. Each player has a separate timer, and refreshes do not extend it.

If teammates remain in the party after the match, or DAY1 was not detected, click **Show teammates** in settings to open the window manually.

Allow seven days between comments to the same player. Keep Steam and the game running when posting. If the result is uncertain, click the name to check the profile. If the player does not allow comments, you can view their profile directly.

Runtime status appears at the bottom of the settings page. The commendation window’s position, opacity, and size can be adjusted separately.
